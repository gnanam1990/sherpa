# Stage 4 P2: ALERT Intent

**Date:** 2026-05-15

## Summary
Added ALERT intent for price, balance, and health-factor trigger notifications.

## What changed

### Parser (`packages/core/src/parser.ts`)
- 4 regex patterns: price alert, balance alert, health-factor alert, cross detection
- Supports operators: `>`, `<`, `>=`, `<=`, `==`, `cross`
- Slots: `conditionType`, `asset`, `comparison`, `threshold`

### Tests (`packages/core/src/index.test.ts`)
- 6 ALERT parser tests covering all 4 patterns plus case-insensitivity and `>=` operator
- Verifies slot extraction for asset, comparison, threshold, and conditionType

### Documentation
- Decision record: `docs/sherpa/decisions/2026-05-15-alert-stage-4.md`
- This devlog

## Design notes
- Price/balance alerts poll on 5-minute cron; health-factor on 1-minute cron (emergency)
- "cross" comparison detects threshold transitions using last_value
- Rate limited to 1 trigger per 6 hours per alert to avoid notification spam
- Triggered alerts notify only — no auto-execution of follow-up intents
