# ADR: Risk Management (Stage 9 P3)

**Date:** 2026-05-15
**Status:** Accepted
**Stage:** 9 — Risk Management

## Context

Sherpa needs risk assessment capabilities so users can understand portfolio exposure, concentration risk, and potential vulnerabilities. Users should be able to express risk-related intents like "check my portfolio risk", "show my exposure", or "hedge my portfolio" as natural language.

## Decision

### Risk Module (`packages/tools/src/risk/`)

- **Assessor** (`assessor.ts`): Three functions:
  - `assessPortfolioRisk(address, deps)` → returns `PortfolioRisk` with overall risk level, score, factors, and recommendations.
  - `getExposureBreakdown(address, deps)` → returns `ExposureBreakdown` with by-chain, by-protocol, and by-asset breakdowns.
  - `calculateRiskScore(factors)` → pure function; maps severity levels to a 0–100 score.
- **Types** (`types.ts`): `PortfolioRisk`, `RiskFactor`, `ExposureBreakdown`, `RiskDeps`.

### Parser Integration

Four regex patterns in `parseDeterministic()`:

| Pattern | Intent | Slot |
|---------|--------|------|
| `check my portfolio risk` | RISK | riskAction=check |
| `show my exposure` | RISK | riskAction=exposure |
| `hedge my portfolio` | RISK | riskAction=hedge |
| `set risk alert if <condition>` | RISK | riskAction=alert |

`RISK` added to `VALID_INTENTS` allowlist for LLM fallback.

### Executor

`planRisk()` in `executor.ts` dispatches by `riskAction`:
- `check` — runs `assessPortfolioRisk()`, returns a summary card.
- `exposure` — runs `getExposureBreakdown()`, returns exposure card.
- `hedge` — suggests hedge strategies.
- `alert` — creates a risk-conditioned alert.

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Merge RISK into ALERT** — rejected; risk assessment is a distinct capability with its own data model.
2. **Separate intents per action** — rejected; `RISK` with `riskAction` slot keeps intent surface small.

## Consequences

- +1 test file (`assessor.test.ts`), +4 parser tests, +2 doc files.
- Risk calculations return stub data; real on-chain integration deferred to Stage 10.
- `calculateRiskScore` is pure math — no external dependencies.
